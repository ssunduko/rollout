Typography
Hero headlines: Inter Display Bold, letter-spacing -1%
Section headings: Inter Bold
Labels and UI text: Inter Medium / Semi Bold
Body text: Inter Regular

Colors
Primary (accent, headline highlight, pills, links): #5b4cf5
CTA button: #10b981

Layout
