# Smart Scheduling

## What it is
Smart Scheduling lets customers book a meeting without leaving the text
conversation. The assistant offers open times from the team's connected
calendars, the customer picks one by replying, and the meeting is created,
confirmed and reminded automatically.

## Who it is for
Sales and support teams that book demos, consultations or service visits over
text and lose leads in the back-and-forth of finding a time.

## Capabilities

### Offer open times in the conversation
The rep (or the AI assistant) clicks "Suggest times" in the composer. A card
with three open slots — e.g. "Tue 10:00 AM", "Tue 2:30 PM", "Wed 9:00 AM" — is
inserted into the reply. The customer answers "2" or taps a slot.

### Connected team calendars
In Settings > Scheduling, teams connect Google or Microsoft calendars and set
working hours, buffer time and meeting length. Round-robin assigns the meeting
to the next available rep.

### Automatic confirmations and reminders
When a slot is picked the meeting is created, a confirmation message is sent,
and reminders go out 24 hours and 1 hour before. Statuses: Booked, Reminded,
Rescheduled, No-show.

### Booking rules per inbox
Each inbox can have its own meeting types (Demo 30 min, Onboarding 45 min),
availability and the questions asked before booking.

### Coming soon: rescheduling by text
Customers will be able to reply "reschedule" to get new times.
